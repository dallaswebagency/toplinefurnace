Furnace and AC Cleaning

  • Your furnace and air conditioning system work year-round to keep your home comfortable. Over time, dust, debris and contaminants build up in the system, reducing air cleaning efficiency and affecting the quality of your indoor air.

    Read more

  • We thoroughly clean your home’s entire forced-air ductwork. Using powerful negative air machines and specialized agitation tools, we remove dust, allergens, mold spores and debris from supply lines, return ducts, trunk lines and the furnace cabinet.

    Read more

  • Over time your air ducts collect dust, debris, pet dander and other contaminants that circulate through your living space. Our professional air duct cleaning removes these hidden pollutants to restore proper airflow and help your HVAC system run more efficiently.

    Read more

  • Our central vacuum cleaning service makes sure your entire vacuum system is cleaned and maintained properly. We remove blockages, clean filters and restore strong suction to keep your home cleaner and healthier.

    Read more

  • A clogged dryer vent reduces your dryer’s efficiency and is a leading cause of house fires. We clear built-up lint and obstructions so your clothes dry faster, your energy bills drop and your home stays safer.

    Read more

  • Our air purification solutions tackle airborne contaminants at the source. Whether it’s allergies, odours or lingering dust, we install the right high-efficiency system for your space to reduce pollutants and support long-term respiratory health.

    Read more

  • Dirty carpets hold soil, dust and dust mites that can lead to allergies, asthma and other respiratory problems, and the grit damages the fibres. We stop that damage and make your carpets and rugs look like new.
